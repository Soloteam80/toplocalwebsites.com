COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear, straightforward answers about our websites, pricing, domains, ownership, hosting and ongoing support.

How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start from €790, with higher-tier options available for businesses that need more pages, advanced features, multilingual content or additional marketing support.Every project is clearly scoped before work begins, so you know exactly what is included and what any optional extras will cost.There are no unexpected charges.

What is a static website and why is it a good choice?

A static website is built using lightweight technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript, without relying on a constantly running database or complex content-management system. This makes the website fast, reliable, easier to maintain and less exposed to common security issues associated with vulnerable plugins and outdated software. Static websites are particularly suitable for local businesses that need a professional online presence, clear information, service pages, contact options and strong performance.

How is TopLocalWebsites different from Wix or Squarespace?

Website builders provide general-purpose templates that businesses must adapt to fit their needs. We create professionally designed, conversion-focused websites tailored to your business, services, customers and local market. Our websites are lightweight, fast and built around helping visitors take action — whether that means calling, booking, requesting a quote or visiting your business. We also avoid unnecessary platform complexity and monthly website-builder subscriptions.

Are there any ongoing costs after the website is completed?

The website itself is a one-time project cost. Depending on your setup, you may have separate costs for domain registration, hosting, maintenance, premium services or optional updates. We explain all recurring and optional costs clearly before the project begins.

Is hosting included in the website price?

Website hosting is not automatically included in every package. You can either use our optional managed hosting and maintenance service or host your website with a provider of your choice. Our managed option is designed for businesses that prefer us to handle deployment, technical configuration and ongoing support.

Can I host the website somewhere else?

Yes. You own your website and are free to host it with another provider. We can provide the website files and assist with the migration or deployment process where appropriate. Choosing our hosting service is simply a convenient option for clients who prefer us to handle the technical side.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes, although static websites do not normally include a traditional visual editor like WordPress. You can request content updates from us whenever you need them, such as changing opening hours, updating prices, adding services, replacing photos, updating menus or adding promotions. We offer affordable on-demand updates and maintenance options, so you do not need to manage the technical files yourself.

Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer optional ongoing maintenance and support for businesses that need content updates, seasonal changes, image replacements, technical assistance or hosting support. You can choose occasional on-demand updates or an ongoing maintenance plan, depending on your needs.

Do I fully own my website and domain?

Yes. You retain ownership of your website content, business information, images and domain. If you choose our hosting service, you are still not locked into a permanent platform lease. You can request your website files and move to another hosting provider if you decide to do so.

Do you provide domain registration and hosting?

Yes. We can assist with domain registration, DNS configuration, hosting setup and website deployment. Hosting is an optional service. You may either use our recommended hosting and maintenance solution or choose an external provider independently. Domain registration fees and hosting/maintenance fees are separate and will be clearly explained before the project begins.

Does website hosting include business email and contact-form setup?

Website hosting and business email are separate services. Our website hosting covers the hosting and operation of your website, while your professional email mailbox is provided through your chosen email service, such as Neo, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or another email provider.

We can help configure your domain email and connect your website contact form so enquiries are delivered directly to your existing business inbox. This means you can continue using your preferred email provider without needing to change your email system.

If you do not yet have a professional business email address, we can also advise you on suitable options and help with the initial setup.

How do WhatsApp buttons, phone links and contact forms work?

We can integrate practical contact tools such as click-to-call phone buttons, WhatsApp contact buttons, email links, Google Maps directions, booking links, contact forms and social media links. The exact functionality depends on the business requirements and the services being used. Before launch, we test the main contact features to ensure they work correctly on desktop and mobile devices.

How long does it take to build my website?

Delivery time depends on the size and complexity of the project. Typical timeframes are approximately 3–5 business days for Starter websites, 7–10 business days for Growth websites, and 10–14 business days for Elite or highly customised projects. The timeframe begins once we have received the required business information, content, images and feedback.

Can I move my website to another hosting provider?

Yes. You retain ownership of your website and are free to move it to another hosting provider. We do not require you to remain on our hosting service.

Can you help with Google Business Profile and local SEO?

Yes. Depending on your selected package, we can help improve your website’s local SEO foundations, business information, location signals, structured data and Google Business Profile presence. These improvements are designed to make your business easier for potential customers to discover online.

Do you work with businesses outside Ireland?

Yes. We work with businesses in Ireland, Italy and other locations internationally. Because the entire process can be handled remotely, we can work with businesses regardless of their location.

What happens after my website goes live?

Once your website is approved, we deploy it to the selected hosting provider, connect the domain if required, check the main pages and contact functions, and make sure the website is ready for visitors. Additional maintenance and updates can be arranged whenever needed.

How is the free consultation conducted?

Our free initial consultation takes place remotely through Google Meet, WhatsApp Call, Skype or Microsoft Teams, depending on your preference and availability. We discuss your business, website requirements and goals, answer your questions and explain the most suitable options for your needs. There is no obligation to purchase.
