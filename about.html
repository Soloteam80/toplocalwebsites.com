ABOUT TOP LOCAL WEBSITES

Who We Are & What We Believe

TopLocalWebsites is an independent web design studio helping local businesses build fast, professional and search-friendly websites that make it easier for customers to discover, understand and contact them. We believe great websites should be simple, transparent and built around real business needs — without unnecessary complexity.

OUR STORY

Built Independently.
Focused on What Matters.

TopLocalWebsites was founded by an Italian web designer and digital creator who wanted to offer local businesses a simpler, more practical alternative to complicated and expensive website solutions.

Over the years, we noticed a common problem: many small businesses were using websites that were outdated, difficult to maintain or not clearly designed around the needs of their customers.

Too often, businesses were left with unnecessary complexity, unclear pricing and websites that did not communicate their services effectively.

That led to the creation of Top Local Websites — an independent web studio focused on clarity, performance, practical functionality and transparent pricing.

We create fast, clean, conversion-focused websites that help businesses present themselves professionally, communicate their services clearly and make it easier for potential customers to get in touch.

Today, TopLocalWebsites works remotely with businesses across Europe and internationally, creating websites in English and Italian for companies that want a stronger, more effective online presence.

OUR MISSION

Helping Local Businesses Compete Online

We believe every local business deserves a website that reflects the quality of its services. Our mission is to create fast, professional websites that build confidence, support online discoverability and make it easier for visitors to take the next step — without unnecessary long-term commitments or technical complexity.
